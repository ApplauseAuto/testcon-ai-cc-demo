# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused
and in the original packaging. Gift cards and personalised items are excluded.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10
business days. Store credit is issued instantly if the customer prefers it.

## Partial refunds

Damaged or incomplete returns may receive a partial refund at the discretion of
the support team, documented in the customer's ticket.
